### You're talented. You're ambitious. But are you *visible*?

You're doing great work. You know you have something valuable to offer — not just to your team or your company, but to the world.

But here's the problem: the best opportunities don't go to the most talented people. They go to the most *visible* ones.

The hiring manager in London? She's looking for talent on LinkedIn — and she's never heard of you.

The startup founder looking for a collaborator? He found someone else. Not because they were better — but because they showed up where he was looking.

The conference organiser putting together next year's speaker lineup? She's browsing profiles and posts — and yours isn't there.

**Being good at what you do isn't enough anymore. You need to be known for what you do.**

No. This guide is designed to work whether you're starting from zero or refining what you already have.

No. The principles and frameworks apply across industries — from tech and marketing to finance, consulting, design, and beyond. The exercises help you customise everything to your specific field.

**How long will it take to see results?**
Personal branding is a long game. You'll have a complete brand strategy and optimised profiles within the first week. Visible momentum typically builds within 60-90 days of consistent execution.

**Is this just about LinkedIn?**
LinkedIn features prominently because it's the highest-leverage platform for most professionals. But the strategies apply to any platform — Twitter/X, personal websites, newsletters, and more.
