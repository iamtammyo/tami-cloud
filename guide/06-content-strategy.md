# Chapter 6: Content Strategy

Your personal brand becomes real when you start creating content. It's the bridge between having a positioning and being *known* for it.

But content without a strategy is just noise. This chapter will help you build a content engine that's sustainable, strategic, and aligned with the brand you've built.

---

## Why Content Is the Engine of Your Brand

Content is how you demonstrate your expertise instead of just claiming it. It's the difference between saying "I'm a great content strategist" and *showing* people how you think about content strategy.

Here's what strategic content does for your brand:

- **Builds authority.** Every piece of content that shares your expertise reinforces your positioning.
- **Creates trust.** When people consistently see valuable insights from you, they start trusting your judgement — before they've ever worked with you.
- **Generates opportunities.** Content is discoverable. A single post, article, or thread can reach someone you'd never have access to through traditional networking.
- **Compounds over time.** Your best content keeps working for you long after you publish it. It gets shared, resurfaces in searches, and builds your reputation even while you sleep.

## The Content Pillars Framework

Don't try to post about everything. Instead, define 3-5 **content pillars** — core topics that align with your brand and that you can create around consistently.

### How to Choose Your Pillars

Your pillars should sit at the intersection of:
- **What you know deeply** (your expertise)
- **What your audience cares about** (their challenges and aspirations)
- **What reinforces your positioning** (the topic you want to be known for)

### Example Content Pillars

For a personal branding strategist targeting early-career African professionals:

| Pillar | Description | Example Topics |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Personal Branding 101 | Foundational concepts and how-tos | "How to write a LinkedIn headline that works," "5 signs your personal brand needs an overhaul" |
| Career Visibility | Standing out in global markets | "Why your work doesn't speak for itself," "How to get noticed by international recruiters" |
| Content Creation | Practical advice on building a content habit | "What to post when you don't know what to post," "My content workflow from idea to publish" |
| Lessons & Stories | Personal experiences and insights | "What I learned from my first viral post," "The mistake that taught me the most about branding" |

## Content Formats That Build Authority

Not all content is created equal. Here are the formats that work best for personal branding:

### The Insight Post
Share a specific lesson, observation, or framework from your experience. These posts show how you think.

*Structure: Hook → Context → Insight → Takeaway*

### The How-To
Teach your audience something practical. Be generous with your knowledge — it builds trust and positions you as an expert.

*Structure: Problem → Step-by-step solution → Result*

### The Story
Share a personal or professional experience that illustrates a principle. Stories are the most memorable form of content.

*Structure: Situation → Conflict/Challenge → Resolution → Lesson*

### The Opinion
Take a clear stance on something in your industry. Opinions spark conversation and show you have a point of view.

*Structure: Contrarian statement → Why you believe it → Evidence → Call to reflection*

### The Curated List
Share resources, tools, books, or recommendations. Curation shows taste and helps your audience — both build your brand.

*Structure: Context for why this matters → List of recommendations → Your take on each*

## Building a Sustainable Content Habit

The biggest risk with content isn't creating bad content — it's stopping. Consistency matters more than perfection. Here's how to build a habit that lasts:

### Start With a Frequency You Can Sustain

| Level | Frequency | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Minimum viable | 1 post per week | Those just starting out or with demanding schedules |
| Consistent | 2-3 posts per week | Building momentum and growing an audience |
| Accelerated | 4-5 posts per week | Rapid growth when content is a top priority |

Start at a level you can maintain for 90 days without burning out. You can always increase later.

### The Content Batching Method

Instead of creating content daily (which is exhausting), batch your content creation:

1. **Capture ideas constantly.** Keep a running note on your phone. Ideas come at random — capture them when they do.
2. **Batch creation weekly.** Set aside 1-2 hours per week to write multiple pieces at once. You'll find it easier to stay in flow.
3. **Schedule in advance.** Use a scheduling tool or LinkedIn's built-in scheduler to queue posts. This frees you from having to post in real-time.
4. **Review and refine.** At the end of each month, review what performed well and double down on those topics and formats.

### Dealing With Writer's Block

When you're stuck, use these prompts:

- What question did someone ask me recently that I could answer in a post?
- What mistake have I made that others could learn from?
- What's a common misconception in my industry that I could address?
- What advice would I give to someone starting where I was 3 years ago?
- What tool, book, or resource has changed how I work — and why?

## Measuring What Matters

Don't get obsessed with vanity metrics. Focus on these indicators:

### Signals That Your Content Is Working

- **Engagement quality** — Are people leaving thoughtful comments, not just likes?
- **DMs and messages** — Are people reaching out because of your content?
- **New connections** — Are the right people (your target audience) finding and following you?
- **Inbound opportunities** — Are you receiving job inquiries, collaboration requests, or speaking invitations?
- **Content longevity** — Are people referencing or sharing your older posts?

### Signals to Adjust

- Consistent low engagement across multiple posts (not just one or two)
- Attracting an audience that doesn't match your target
- Feeling like you're performing rather than sharing authentically
- Burnout or dread around content creation

If you're seeing these signals, revisit your pillars, format, or frequency. Something needs to shift.

## Chapter Summary

- Content is the engine that turns your positioning into recognition and authority.
- Define 3-5 content pillars that sit at the intersection of your expertise, your audience's needs, and your positioning.
- Use a mix of formats: insight posts, how-tos, stories, opinions, and curated lists.
- Start with a sustainable frequency and use batching to stay consistent.
- Measure quality of engagement and inbound opportunities, not just likes and followers.
