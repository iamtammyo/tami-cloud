# 90-Day Brand Building Plan

Your phased action plan for the first 90 days of building (or rebuilding) your personal brand. This plan breaks the process into three phases so you can build momentum without burning out.

---

## Before You Start

Make sure you've completed these prerequisites:

- [ ] Completed the Personal Brand Audit (`toolkit/personal-brand-audit.md`)
- [ ] Defined your brand foundation — values, strengths, story (Chapter 2)
- [ ] Identified your target audience (Chapter 3)
- [ ] Written your positioning statement (Chapter 4)
- [ ] Chosen your primary platform (Chapter 5)

If you haven't done these, start there. This plan assumes you have your strategy in place and are ready to execute.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

**Goal:** Set up your presence, start creating content, and build the habit.

### Week 1: Profile Overhaul

| Task | Done? |
|------|-------|
| Update LinkedIn profile photo | [ ] |
| Create and upload a branded banner image | [ ] |
| Rewrite your LinkedIn headline using the positioning formula | [ ] |
| Rewrite your LinkedIn About section | [ ] |
| Update your Experience section with impact-focused descriptions | [ ] |
| Add 3-5 items to your Featured section | [ ] |
| Update your bio on all other active platforms | [ ] |

### Week 2: Content Setup

| Task | Done? |
|------|-------|
| Define your 3-5 content pillars | [ ] |
| Choose your publishing frequency (start sustainable) | [ ] |
| Set up a content idea capture system (notes app, doc, etc.) | [ ] |
| Brainstorm 10-15 content ideas across your pillars | [ ] |
| Write and publish your first piece of content | [ ] |
| Schedule your weekly content creation time block | [ ] |

### Week 3: Start Engaging

| Task | Done? |
|------|-------|
| Identify 10 people in your space to follow and engage with | [ ] |
| Leave thoughtful comments on 5 posts per day | [ ] |
| Publish your second and third pieces of content | [ ] |
| Join 1-2 relevant communities (Slack groups, forums, etc.) | [ ] |
| Send 2 warm connection requests with personalised notes | [ ] |

### Week 4: Build the Rhythm

| Task | Done? |
|------|-------|
| Publish content according to your chosen frequency | [ ] |
| Continue daily engagement (comments, replies, shares) | [ ] |
| Send 3 outreach DMs using the networking templates | [ ] |
| Do a mini-review: what type of content is getting traction? | [ ] |
| Adjust your approach based on what you're learning | [ ] |

### Phase 1 Review Questions

1. Did I publish content every week? ___
2. Am I showing up consistently on my primary platform? ___
3. What type of content performed best? ___
4. What felt natural? What felt forced? ___
5. What will I adjust for Phase 2? ___

## Quick Reference: Weekly Minimums

No matter what phase you're in, aim for these minimums every week:

| Activity | Weekly Minimum |
|----------|---------------|
| Publish content | 1 post (more if sustainable) |
| Engage with others' content | 5 thoughtful comments per day |
| Direct outreach | 2-3 messages per week |
| Community participation | 1 meaningful interaction |
| Content idea capture | 3-5 new ideas noted |
