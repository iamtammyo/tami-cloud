# Brand Statement Worksheet

This worksheet walks you through building your personal brand statement step by step. By the end, you'll have a clear, compelling statement you can use in your LinkedIn headline, bio, website, and introductions.

---

## Step 1: Your Role or Identity

How do you describe what you do? Go beyond your job title — think about the *function* you serve.

**Job title:** _______________________________________________

**What I actually do (in plain language):** _______________________________________________

**The identity I want to claim:** _______________________________________________

*Examples: "content strategist," "product designer," "marketing consultant," "career coach," "brand builder," "growth advisor"*

---

## Step 2: Your Audience

Who do you help? Be specific about the type of person — not just their role, but their stage, context, and situation.

**Who I serve:** _______________________________________________

**Their career stage:** _______________________________________________

**Their context (industry, location, situation):** _______________________________________________

*Examples: "early-career professionals in Africa," "B2B SaaS founders," "freelance creatives looking to go global," "mid-career women pivoting into tech"*

---

## Step 3: The Outcome You Deliver

What transformation or result do you help your audience achieve? Focus on the *outcome*, not the process.

**The main outcome I help people achieve:** _______________________________________________

**What changes for them as a result:** _______________________________________________

*Examples: "build a personal brand that attracts global opportunities," "land remote roles at international companies," "turn their expertise into a profitable content business," "communicate their value clearly and confidently"*

---

## Step 4: Your Unique Approach

What makes *your* approach different? This could be a methodology, a perspective, an experience, or a philosophy.

**What I bring that others don't:** _______________________________________________

**My unique angle or philosophy:** _______________________________________________

*Examples: "through authentic storytelling," "by combining data with human psychology," "using frameworks from my experience building brands across three continents," "by focusing on depth over volume"*

---

## Step 5: Your Core Belief

What do you believe about your work, your industry, or your audience that drives everything you do?

**I believe:** _______________________________________________

*Examples: "Your story is your most powerful career asset." "Visibility and talent should go hand in hand." "Clear messaging is the most underrated growth lever." "The world needs more diverse voices in positions of influence."*

---

## Step 6: Assemble Your Brand Statement

Now combine your answers into one or two sentences:

### Formula A: The Positioning Statement

> I'm a [Step 1: identity] who helps [Step 2: audience] [Step 3: outcome] through [Step 4: unique approach].

**Your statement:**

> _______________________________________________
> _______________________________________________

### Formula B: The Extended Version (with belief)

> I'm a [Step 1: identity] who helps [Step 2: audience] [Step 3: outcome] through [Step 4: unique approach]. I believe [Step 5: core belief].

**Your statement:**

> _______________________________________________
> _______________________________________________
> _______________________________________________

---

## Step 7: The Gut Check

Read your statement out loud. Then answer:

- [ ] Does it sound like *me* — not like everyone else in my field?
- [ ] Would my ideal audience member read this and think, "this person gets me"?
- [ ] Could I say this confidently in a conversation?
- [ ] Does it make clear what I do, for whom, and why it matters?

If you checked all four, you have a strong working statement. If not, go back and refine the specific step that feels off.

---

## Step 8: Put It to Work

Use your brand statement in these places:

- [ ] **LinkedIn headline** — Use Formula A (or a shortened version)
- [ ] **LinkedIn About section** — Open with Formula B, then expand
- [ ] **Twitter/X bio** — Adapt to fit the character limit
- [ ] **Email signature** — Add a one-line version
- [ ] **Website or portfolio** — Use as your hero text
- [ ] **In-person introductions** — Practice saying it naturally

---

## Remember

Your brand statement is a living document. As your career evolves, your audience shifts, or your perspective deepens, come back and update it. The goal isn't to write the perfect statement once — it's to have a clear one *now* that you can refine over time.
