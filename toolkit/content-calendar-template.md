# Content Calendar Template

Use this template to plan your content week by week. Consistency is what builds a brand — and a calendar makes consistency manageable.

---

## How to Use This Calendar

1. **Choose your frequency.** Start with what you can sustain for 90 days.
2. **Assign pillars to days.** Rotate through your content pillars so your feed stays varied.
3. **Fill in topics during your weekly batch session.** Spend 1-2 hours per week planning and drafting.
4. **Track performance.** Note what performs well so you can do more of it.

## Content Format Guide

Rotate through these formats to keep your feed varied:

| Format | Best For | Frequency |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Insight Post | Sharing a lesson or observation | 1-2x per week |
| How-To | Teaching something practical | 1x per week |
| Story | Making a point through personal experience | 1x per week |
| Opinion | Sparking conversation with a strong take | 1-2x per month |
| Curated List | Sharing resources, tools, or recommendations | 1-2x per month |

## Pro Tips

- **Batch on Sundays.** Dedicate 1-2 hours to writing the week's content. It's faster when you're in flow.
- **Repurpose your best content.** A high-performing post can become a thread, a newsletter, a carousel, or a short video.
- **Engage before and after posting.** Spend 10-15 minutes engaging with others' content before you post, and reply to every comment on your own.
- **Don't break the chain.** Consistency matters more than perfection. A "good enough" post published on time beats a perfect post that never goes live.
